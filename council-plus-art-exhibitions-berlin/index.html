COUNCIL+: Contemporary Art Exhibitions in Berlin

Co-Founder & Director, COUNCIL+ · Berlin · 2021–2025 · Gallery Direction · Exhibition Production · Art Direction · Communication · Kunstausstellungen in Berlin

 COUNCIL+ is a contemporary art gallery in Berlin and an international off-location exhibition project that I co-founded and directed. As director I was responsible for developing the gallery, producing the exhibitions, art direction and visual identity, communication and partnerships.

 For my brand work, COUNCIL+ is a laboratory for experiential formats: every exhibition is a brand experience in miniature – a concept, a space, an audience and a story that have to come together on opening night and hold up for weeks. The exhibitions were curated by Tim Rosenbaum, in part together with guest curators. A selection:


PROLOGUE: Chlorophyllic Chorus

Group exhibition · 2024 · COUNCIL+, Berlin

 The opening chapter of an ongoing exhibition series. Twelve artists trace how human life and thought are rooted in the cycles of nature – growth, decay, photosynthesis, metabolism – and how our bodies and minds keep time with the rhythms of the living world.







With Arhun Aksakal, Carlotta Drinkewitz, Ruben Einsmann, Jason Gringler, Alexander Iezzi, Jeewi Lee, Michael Sailstorfer, Jura Shust, Hannah Rose Stewart, Jordi Theler, Anne de Vries and Kay Yoon · Curated by Tim Rosenbaum · Photos: Jonathan Joosten


NOWHERE II

Group exhibition · 2024 · COUNCIL+, Berlin · in collaboration with 032c

 An exhibition about wellness as the luxury good of our time. From in-flight sleep suites to detox retreats, self-care has become an endless upgrade – a duty and a status symbol at once. The artists look at how wellness and luxury are negotiated in art and culture, and what this says about the absurdities of today’s creative industries.









With Eliza Douglas, Erwan Sene, Amalia Ulman, Varg²™ and Jan Vorisek · Curated by Claire Koron Elat, Shelly Lea Reich and Tim Rosenbaum · Photos: Jonathan Joosten


FROM…WITH LOVE

Solo exhibition of Neckar Doll · 2024 · COUNCIL+, Berlin

 A solo exhibition that unfolds like a near-death dream: a butterfly-shaped opening of light widens until time and space dissolve, and familiar figures – a cowgirl, a witch, a knight – appear as shadows. Hats and helmets resting on bones, a butterfly caught in a cone of light and moving images turn the gallery into a quiet passage between farewell and return.









Neckar Doll (*1995, Mulhouse) · Curated by Tim Rosenbaum · Photos: Jonathan Joosten


THE FALCON CANNOT HEAR THE FALCONER

Group exhibition · 2024 · COUNCIL+, Berlin

 Borrowing its title from W. B. Yeats’ poem The Second Coming (1919), the exhibition looks at how we retreat from an overwhelming world into identity, self-focus and narcissism. The works build distance and shelter – and through their materials expose the vulnerability and isolation behind the armour.









With Blackhaine, Julian-Jakob Kneer, Arthur Marie, Marie Matusz, Hannah Rose Stewart and Lukas Stöver · Curated by Tim Rosenbaum · Photos: Jonathan Joosten


FATHERS CHARIOT / THE FALL

Group exhibition · 2023 · Off-location at von Racknitz + Baer Gallery, Berlin

 Named after Phaeton, who begged his father Helios to let him steer the sun chariot for a single day – and fell. The exhibition reads the myth as a portrait of hubris in privileged Western societies and as a reminder that art always outgrows the artist. It asks visitors to consider the gap between making an impression and trying to make one.









With Sally von Rosen, Claire Barrow, Billie Clarken, Fjorsk and Nadine Lohof · Curated by Tim Rosenbaum · Photos: Jonathan Joosten
